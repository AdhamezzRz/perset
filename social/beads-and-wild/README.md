# Per Set — A Tale in Beads & Stitches of the Wild (motion pack)

Twelve animations, six for each collection:

- 3 animated feed posts per collection, 1080 × 1350 (4:5) MP4;
- 3 Stories per collection, 1080 × 1920 (9:16) MP4.

Everything is silent, so music is picked in-app. Every post loops cleanly, and every story leaves room for a native sticker (poll, quiz, link).

**The plates are always the real product photos.** Every plate is cut from the shop's own photos (perset.shop product images):

- the vinyl spin;
- the runway walk;
- the zoom from space;
- the glowing beads;
- the stories.

The one Kling clip, the breakfast speedrun, starts from the real flat-lay photo. The plate is restored frame by frame wherever nothing covers it.

The beads that light up are the plate's own rim beads, found in the photo. There are about 151 to 155 of them depending on how they are counted, which is why the quiz says "150 and up".
