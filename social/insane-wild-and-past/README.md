# Per Set: Stitches of the Wild & Pretty Past, the insane pack

Twelve pieces, six per collection: 3 Reels, 2 posts and 1 carousel each.

**The plates are always the real product photos.** Every plate is cut from the shop’s photography and composited into the scene. In the two Kling clips (the street cat and the auction), the real plate is restored frame by frame wherever nothing passes in front of it. The plate’s full border is visible in every piece.

All Reels are silent, so music is picked in-app.
