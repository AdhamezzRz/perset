# Per Set — Stitches of the Wild & Pretty Past

Two collection drops, each with 3 Reels, 3 feed posts and 1 carousel.
Feed posts and carousels are 1080 × 1350 (4:5). Reels are 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps and silent, so music is picked in-app.

## The rule behind every frame: the plates are real

Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5 and Kling 3.0) built the worlds: the Zamalek wall, the beaches, the nish, the peacock, the 1970s family photos.
In every generated scene, the plate was then **replaced with the real Per Set product photo**:

- the plate was located with GrabCut;
- the real cutout was warped onto it;
- it was relit from the scene and graded to match.

In the Kling videos this is done frame by frame (`vtool.py`). The real plate is re-aligned and pasted back wherever nothing covers it. Hands, glasses and the door pass in front of it naturally.

The leopards, parasols, peacock and roses you see are the actual designs. The reels with no generation at all use only the real photos:

- Stitches of the Wild Reel 1;
- Pretty Past Reel 2;
- Pretty Past Reel 1's spaghetti shot, which is Kling animating the real lifestyle photo.

Voice rules from the brief still hold: never "luxury", and none of "hand painted / hand drawn / fired in small runs / no two alike".

## Source

`src/` holds the builders. The main ones:

- `plate_tool.py` swaps in the real plate;
- `vtool.py` restores the plate frame by frame and mirrors the symmetry shots;
- `reel.py` and `endcard.py` assemble the reels;
- `b_*.py` build the posts and carousels;
- `r_*.py` build the reels.
